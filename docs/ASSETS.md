# ZERO MILE — 3D asset register

Every 3D model in the game is an original asset authored for ZERO MILE, in code, in this repository. No model,
texture or material was downloaded, extracted from another game, generated by a third-party service or copied from
a real vehicle design. There is no third-party 3D content to attribute.

## How the assets are made

Authored geometry, not primitives: each asset is modelled from profiles, lofts, lathes, extrusions and bevelled
parts in `src/assets/`, built once, cached, and drawn through a single surface atlas material.

| Piece | File | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface atlas | `src/assets/atlas.ts` | 32 surfaces (paint, glass, trim, chrome, rubber, lamps, carbon, concrete, steel, rust, wood, brick, foliage…) in an 8×4 data texture: colour, clear coat, roughness, metalness, emission. One material per car, one per world segment. Live light colours (head, tail, accent), dirt, dents and soot are shader uniforms. |
| Modelling kit | `src/assets/meshkit.ts` | Chamfered boxes, lathes, tubes, extrusions, creased normals; three detail levels (hero, standard, far). |
| Car bodies | `src/assets/vehicles/body.ts` | Lofted bodies from design lines (roof/hood/deck heights, beltline, tumblehome, plan rounding), wheel arches cut into the sides with flares and fender crests, real panel gaps, side vents, fascias (grilles, lamps, bumpers, diffusers, plates, exhausts), mirrors, pickup beds. |
| Wheels | `src/assets/vehicles/wheel.ts` | Round tyres with shoulders, rim barrel and lip, brake disc and caliper, 5/6/mesh/heavy spokes or dished steel wheels, hub caps. |
| Vehicle parts | `src/assets/vehicles/parts.ts` | Wings, roll cages, ram blades, light bars, turrets, EMP rig, side armour, scoops, stacks, spare wheels, racks, barrels, push bars, fins, cargo boxes, truck chassis, tanks. |
| Vehicle catalogue | `src/assets/vehicles/catalog.ts` | Every vehicle as a design: 4 player cars, 13 traffic variants, 7 enemy classes + elite, 5 bosses. |
| Props | `src/assets/kit/props.ts` | 48 street, roadworks, industrial and gameplay props. |
| Building kit | `src/assets/kit/buildings.ts` | Facade relief placed on the facade-texture grid: balconies, AC units, down-pipes, cornices, plinths, slab edges, fins, pilasters, pipe runs, roller-door frames, fire escapes, entrances, canopies, roof plant. |
| Industrial kit | `src/assets/kit/industry.ts` | Storage tanks, silos, cooling tower, chimney, refinery column, pipe rack, pipe bridge, gantry and tower cranes, flare, hopper. |
| Infrastructure kit | `src/assets/kit/infra.ts` | Highway piers, deck girders, cable-stayed pylons, bridge parapets, tunnel portals, ribs with jet fans, tunnel wall furniture. |

Brands and words on signs and billboards are invented. No vehicle is a copy of a production car: proportions are
generic for their class and every detail (lamps, grilles, wings, armour) is drawn for this game.

## External libraries considered and not used

CC0 asset packs (for example the Kenney and Quaternius vehicle and city kits) were evaluated as a possible base and
not used: their toy-like proportions and flat colours do not fit the stylized-realistic target, they would need a
second material pipeline (and several draw calls per car instead of one), and nothing they offer is missing from
the authored set. If specific CC0 models are wanted later, they can go through the same atlas pipeline; each one
must then get its own row above with source, author, licence, original URL and modifications.
